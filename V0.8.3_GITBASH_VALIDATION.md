## Defect found and corrected

The clean environment revealed an intermittent availability failure during
concurrent audit activity. A verifier could read continuation receipts and the
audit head at different moments, interpreting legitimate progress as rollback.
An additional first-check race existed when one request observed an initialized
empty anchor while another request had appended the first receipt but had not
yet published its local checkpoint.

`ExternalAuditAnchor.snapshot_since()` now returns continuation receipts and
the head under one lock. First-head adoption verifies the signed receipt and
full chain regardless of initialization order. This preserves concurrent
execution without globally serializing business effects.
